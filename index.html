About this archive

This collection uses your original nine-page question PDF plus the additional SPM, ML and Security photographs. SPM and ML session 2019–20 are labelled by their printed exam dates; the extra Security paper has no session printed. It includes all question options, even where the exam asks students to choose a subset.

Answers are independently prepared model study notes in English, not an official answer key or instructor-reviewed marking scheme. Unclear wording and assumptions are identified within answers.

The HCI header says 4th Year 2nd Semester. The source is preserved as uploaded. The SPM term test is a separate collection with its own download. The uploaded 2021–22 term-test solutions PDF is also available separately for Computer Security, SPM, Machine Learning and HCI.

Saved answers and reading progress stay in this browser. No payment or print-order service is configured.
